How we track

AI policy is contested. This tracker's job is to record what governments have actually done, accurately and without taking sides.

What each status means

In effect

Signed or issued and currently operative.

Signed, not yet in effect

Enacted, with an effective date still ahead. Court challenges are noted where they exist.

Proposed

Formally put forward by the White House or a governor, but not law.

Revoked or replaced

No longer operative, kept for the record.

What we include

Executive orders, enacted federal and state laws, and major formal policy documents that specifically address AI. We do not list every bill introduced; most never become law.

How we stay nonpartisan

Each entry describes what an action does, in neutral terms, and links to its source: the official text where available, otherwise a summary from an established law firm or legal publication. We do not rate, praise or criticize policies.

Updates

The tracker is reviewed monthly. Spotted an error or a missing action? Email inquiries@arnovasolutions.com.
